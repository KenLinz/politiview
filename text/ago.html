Angola

Middle Africa, about 31,825,295 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: João Lourenço, People's Movement for the Liberation of Angola, since 23 August 2017 (João Lourenço's record on Wikidata)

The Next Vote

Parliament, 24 August 2027.

How It's Run

The leader of the winning party's list automatically becomes president and has strong powers. The MPLA has governed since independence.

How Voting Works

Voters choose party lists, and seats are shared by vote share. There isn't a separate presidential vote. In 2022 the MPLA won 51 percent and UNITA 44, the closest result in Angola's history, and the opposition challenged the outcome.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Independent from Portugal in 1975, Angola was torn by a 27-year civil war that was also a Cold War battleground, ending in 2002. Critics say oil wealth has been concentrated among a small elite.
