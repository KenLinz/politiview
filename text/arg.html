President, Senate and Chamber of Deputies, 24 October 2027.

How It's Run

Voters elect a president, who runs the government, and a National Congress with two chambers. Argentina has 23 provinces plus Buenos Aires, and each has powers of its own.

How Voting Works

A president wins with 45%, or 40% with a 10-point lead. Otherwise there's a runoff. Half of the lower house is elected every two years, so midterms matter a lot. Voting is compulsory.

How It Got Here

Argentina has been free of Spain since 1816. It grew rich, then swung between Juan Perón's strong political movement and army coups. A dictatorship from 1976 to 1983 made thousands of people disappear. Economic crises have shaped politics ever since democracy came back.
