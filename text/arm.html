Armenia

Western Asia, about 2,957,731 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Nikol Pashinyan, Civil Contract, since 8 May 2018 (Nikol Pashinyan's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Vahagn Khachatryan, Independent, since 13 March 2022 (Vahagn Khachatryan's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. Since a change in 2018, a president picked by parliament has a job that's mostly for show.

How Voting Works

Parties share out seats by their votes, with rules that guarantee a stable majority and seats for minorities. The governing Civil Contract party was reported to have won the June 2026 election.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Armenia is said by tradition to be the first state to adopt Christianity, in 301. Armenia and many historians and countries call the mass killings of Armenians under the Ottoman Empire from 1915 a genocide, which Turkey rejects. It broke from the Soviets in 1991 and fought wars with Azerbaijan over Nagorno-Karabakh. In 2023 Azerbaijan took full control, and almost all of the region's Armenians left. The 2018 Velvet Revolution brought today's leaders.
