Azerbaijan

Western Asia, about 10,023,318 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Ilham Aliyev, New Azerbaijan Party, since 31 October 2003 (Ilham Aliyev's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Ali Asadov, New Azerbaijan Party, since 8 October 2018 (Ali Asadov's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A strong president dominates. Ilham Aliyev took over from his father in 2003, and term limits have been removed. His wife is the vice president.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but international observers say they don't meet democratic standards, and the main opposition parties have refused to take part. Critics say journalists and opponents are often jailed, which the government denies.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Azerbaijan broke from the Soviet Union in 1991 and grew rich from Caspian oil and gas. It took back much of Nagorno-Karabakh in 2020 and the rest in 2023.
