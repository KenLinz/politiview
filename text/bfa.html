Burkina Faso

Western Africa, about 20,321,378 people

Who's In Charge

  • Interim President: Ibrahim Traoré, since 6 October 2022 (Ibrahim Traoré's record on Wikidata)
  • Interim Prime Minister: Jean Emmanuel Ouédraogo, Independent (Jean Emmanuel Ouédraogo's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Captain Ibrahim Traoré has ruled since a coup in September 2022, the second that year, while the army fights jihadist groups. Political parties are heavily restricted.

How Voting Works

Promised elections were pushed back, and the transition was extended by five years in 2024.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • Nobody has to vote.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 20 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 4.5 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 56% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Revolutionary leader Thomas Sankara renamed the country Burkina Faso in 1984. He was killed in a 1987 coup led by Blaise Compaoré, who ruled until a popular uprising in 2014.

Who It Stands With

  • African Union: Nearly every country in Africa, working together on peace and politics.
