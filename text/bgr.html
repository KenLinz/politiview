Bulgaria

Eastern Europe, about 6,975,761 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Rumen Radev, Progressive Bulgaria, since 8 May 2026 (Rumen Radev's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Iliana Iotova, Bulgarian Socialist Party, since 23 January 2026 (Iliana Iotova's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect the National Assembly, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, also elected by voters, is mostly for show.

How Voting Works

Seats are shared by party vote share with a 4% minimum. Fragile coalitions have led to many repeat elections in recent years.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.

Who Takes Part

  • About 39% of registered voters turned out in 2024, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 24% of seats in parliament.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • V-Dem: Electoral Democracy (2025)
  • Freedom House: 74 Out Of 100 (2025)
  • Bertelsmann: Defective Democracy (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 5.7 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 40% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Bulgaria gained independence from the Ottoman Empire in stages, up to 1908. It was a communist state from 1946 until 1989, and joined the EU in 2007.

Who It Stands With

  • NATO: A military alliance. Members promise to defend one another.
  • European Union: European countries that make many of their laws together.
