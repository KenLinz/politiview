Barbados

Caribbean, about 287,025 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Mia Mottley, Barbados Labour Party, since 25 May 2018 (Mia Mottley's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Jeffrey Bostic, Barbados Labour Party (Jeffrey Bostic's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. Barbados became a republic in 2021. A president picked by parliament replaced the British monarch as head of state.

How Voting Works

Local areas elect members by first-past-the-post. Results can be very one-sided: one party has won every seat in several recent elections.

How It Got Here

England took Barbados as a colony in 1627, and its sugar farms relied on enslaved Africans. Slavery ended in the 1830s, and independence came in 1966. Its parliament is one of the oldest in the Americas.
