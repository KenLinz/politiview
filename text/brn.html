Brunei

South-Eastern Asia, about 433,285 people

Who's In Charge

  • Sultan: Hassanal Bolkiah I of Brunei (Hassanal Bolkiah I of Brunei's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

The sultan holds absolute power, serving as both head of state and prime minister. Islamic law plays a large part in its legal system.

How Voting Works

There are no national elections, and there haven't been since 1962. That year an opposition party won every elected seat, a revolt broke out, and British help put it down. Emergency rule followed.

  • Nobody has to vote.

Who Takes Part

  • Women hold about 11% of seats in parliament.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 27 Out Of 100 (2025)

How It Got Here

Once a major regional power, Brunei became a British protectorate. Independent since 1984, it's wealthy from oil and gas.

Who It Stands With

  • ASEAN: Southeast Asian countries cooperating on politics and security.
  • The Commonwealth: Mostly former parts of the British Empire, tied by history and language.
