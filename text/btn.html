Bhutan

Southern Asia, about 763,092 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Tshering Tobgay, People's Democratic Party, since 28 January 2024 (Tshering Tobgay's record on Wikidata)
  • King: Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck, since 6 November 2008 (Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A king is head of state, but an elected parliament and prime minister run the government. Unusually, the king himself pushed the country to democracy, even though many people liked things as they were.

How Voting Works

Voters first choose between parties in a primary round, and then the top two compete in each of the 47 local areas. The upper house is elected without parties, plus five members the king appoints.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

The Wangchuck dynasty has ruled since 1907. Bhutan stayed largely isolated until the 1960s. It's known for measuring 'Gross National Happiness', and held its first national elections in 2007-08.
