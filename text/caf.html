Central African Republic

Middle Africa, about 4,745,185 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Faustin-Archange Touadéra, United Hearts Movement, since 30 March 2016 (Faustin-Archange Touadéra's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Félix Moloua, United Hearts Movement, since 7 February 2022 (Félix Moloua's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

President Faustin-Archange Touadéra runs the government, and a 2023 vote removed term limits. Armed groups hold parts of the country, and Russian military contractors help provide security.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but violence makes voting impossible in some areas, and the opposition has refused to take part in some. Touadéra won a third term in December 2025 with over 70 percent, while the opposition claimed repression.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

It won independence from France in 1960 and has been through many coups. Jean-Bédel Bokassa even declared himself emperor in 1976. Rebels took the capital in 2013, and conflict has driven many people from their homes.
