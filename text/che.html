How It's Run

Switzerland has no single leader. Seven ministers, called the Federal Council, run the country together, and they take turns being president for a year. The biggest parties share these seats by long-standing deal, so the main parties all govern together.

How Voting Works

Voters elect parliament, but also vote directly on laws and constitutional changes, several times a year. Enough signatures can force a national referendum or launch a proposal for a vote. The 26 states, called cantons, hold a lot of power. Women gained the right to vote nationally only in 1971.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Swiss communities first allied in 1291. After a short civil war in 1847, they wrote a federal constitution in 1848. Switzerland has stayed officially neutral in wars for over 200 years.
