Democratic Republic of the Congo

Middle Africa, about 86,790,567 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Félix Tshisekedi, Union for Democracy and Social Progress, since 24 January 2019 (Félix Tshisekedi's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Judith Suminwa, Union for Democracy and Social Progress (Judith Suminwa's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a president and a National Assembly, and provincial assemblies elect the Senate. In the east, armed groups have fought the government for years. They include the M23, which UN experts say Rwanda backs and Rwanda denies.

How Voting Works

The president wins with the most votes in one round. Elections have been disputed, and organizing a vote in a huge country with few roads is very hard.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Belgium's King Leopold ran the Congo as his own property from 1885, with horrors on a huge scale, until Belgium took it over in 1908. After independence in 1960, its first prime minister, Patrice Lumumba, was killed. Mobutu ruled from 1965 until he was overthrown in 1997. Wars followed from 1996 to 2003.
