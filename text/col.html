Colombia

South America, about 50,339,443 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Abelardo de la Espriella, Defenders of the Homeland, since 7 August 2026 (Abelardo de la Espriella's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a president for one four-year term, along with a Senate and a House of Representatives. Courts and independent bodies are very strong here.

How Voting Works

The president needs a majority, or the top two go to a runoff. Congress seats are shared by party vote share. Special seats are set aside for Indigenous and Afro-Colombian communities.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 6.0 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 43% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Colombia won independence from Spain in 1819. A decades-long war between the government, left-wing rebels, right-wing armed groups and drug gangs has killed hundreds of thousands. A 2016 peace deal ended the war with the largest rebel group, but other armed groups remain.
