Comoros

Eastern Africa, about 850,886 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Azali Assoumani, Convention for the Renewal of the Comoros, since 1 January 2016 (Azali Assoumani's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A president runs the government of this island nation. Until 2018 the job moved in turn between the islands. A vote that year ended that, and now a president can serve two five-year terms.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but opposition groups and some observers say they aren't free or fair. In the January 2024 vote for president, official results first gave President Azali about 63 percent. After a review by the Supreme Court, that became about 57 percent.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

Who Takes Part

  • About 66% of registered voters turned out in 2025, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 18% of seats in parliament.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • V-Dem: Electoral Autocracy (2025)
  • Freedom House: 41 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 3.9 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).

How It Got Here

Independent from France since 1975, the Comoros has had more than 20 coups or attempted coups. The island of Mayotte voted in 1974 to remain French and is a French department, but the Comoros still claims it.

Who It Stands With

  • African Union: Nearly every country in Africa, working together on peace and politics.
  • Arab League: Arabic-speaking countries that coordinate on politics.
