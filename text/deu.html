How It's Run

Voters elect the Bundestag, and it chooses a chancellor, who runs the government. A president is the ceremonial head of state. Germany is federal: its 16 states run schools and police, and they have their own say in national law through a second chamber, the Bundesrat.

How Voting Works

Each voter gets two votes: one for a local candidate, and one for a party. The party vote decides how many seats each party gets overall. A party usually needs 5% of the party vote to get in, which keeps very small parties out. Governments are nearly always coalitions.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Germany only became one country in 1871. After World War I, its first democracy collapsed and Hitler took power in 1933. After World War II, it was split into East and West, and West Germany wrote its Basic Law in 1949 with safeguards to stop a dictator rising again. The two sides reunited in 1990.
