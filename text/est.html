Estonia

Northern Europe, about 1,326,590 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Kristen Michal, Estonian Reform Party, since 23 July 2024 (Kristen Michal's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Ülle Madise, Independent, since 12 October 2026 (Ülle Madise's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect the Riigikogu, and the prime minister runs the government. A president is mostly for show. Parliament picks the president, or a special group does if parliament can't agree.

How Voting Works

Seats are shared by party vote share with a 5% minimum. Estonia was the first country to allow online voting in national elections, starting in 2005 for local ones and 2007 for parliament.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 6.4 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 56% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Estonia became independent in 1918, but the Soviet Union took it over in 1940. It won its freedom back in 1991 after a peaceful 'Singing Revolution'. It joined the EU and NATO in 2004.

Who It Stands With

  • NATO: A military alliance. Members promise to defend one another.
  • European Union: European countries that make many of their laws together.
