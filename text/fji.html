Fiji

Melanesia, about 889,953 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Sitiveni Rabuka, People's Alliance, since 24 December 2022 (Sitiveni Rabuka's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Naiqama Lalabalavu, Social Democratic Liberal Party (Naiqama Lalabalavu's record on Wikidata)

The Next Vote

Constitutional referendum, 9 December 2026.

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, picked by parliament, has a job that's mostly for show.

How Voting Works

All voters choose from one nationwide list and seats are shared by party vote share. Supporters say this reduces voting along ethnic lines.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Britain brought workers from India to farm sugar. Tension between Indigenous Fijians and Indo-Fijians was a big part of the story behind coups in 1987 (two), 2000 and 2006. A new constitution came in 2013, and elections were held in 2014.

Who It Stands With
