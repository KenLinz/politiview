How It's Run

France has both a strong president, elected by voters, and a prime minister. The president sets foreign policy and appoints the prime minister. The prime minister runs daily government but must keep the support of the National Assembly. This is called semi-presidential.

How Voting Works

The president is elected in two rounds: if nobody gets a majority, the top two face off two weeks later. The National Assembly is also elected one seat per district, with a second round if nobody wins outright. Local officials, not the public, choose the Senate.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

The 1789 Revolution overthrew the king, and France has swung between republics, empires and kings since. Charles de Gaulle designed today's Fifth Republic in 1958, during a crisis over Algeria. He wanted a stronger leader after years of weak governments that didn't last.
