Georgia

Western Asia, about 3,720,382 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Irakli Kobakhidze, Georgian Dream, since 8 February 2024 (Irakli Kobakhidze's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Mikheil Kavelashvili, People's Power, since 29 December 2024 (Mikheil Kavelashvili's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, whose job is mostly for show, is now picked by a special group rather than by the public.

How Voting Works

Seats are shared by party vote share, with a 5% minimum. The opposition and many observers rejected the 2024 results, alleging fraud and pressure, while the government says the vote was fair. Months of protests followed after the government paused talks on joining the European Union.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 5.5 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 58% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Georgia broke from the Soviet Union in 1991. The peaceful Rose Revolution of 2003 removed the old leaders. After a short war with Russia in 2008, two regions, Abkhazia and South Ossetia, came under Russian-backed control. Georgia and most countries see them as Georgian land, while Russia recognizes them as independent.
