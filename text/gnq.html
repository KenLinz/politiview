Equatorial Guinea

Middle Africa, about 1,355,986 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Teodoro Obiang, Democratic Party of Equatorial Guinea, since 3 August 1979 (Teodoro Obiang's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Manuel Osa Nsue Nsua, Democratic Party of Equatorial Guinea, since 17 August 2024 (Manuel Osa Nsue Nsua's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Teodoro Obiang has ruled since 1979, making him one of the world's longest-serving leaders. His son is vice president, and the PDGE party dominates.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but they are described by Freedom House and other observers as neither free nor fair. Obiang wins with over 90 percent, and the opposition has been barred or jailed, according to rights groups.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

It won independence from Spain in 1968. Francisco Macías Nguema, widely described as a brutal dictator, ran it until his nephew Obiang overthrew him in 1979. Large amounts of oil were found in the 1990s.
