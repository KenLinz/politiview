Greece

Southern Europe, about 10,716,322 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Kyriakos Mitsotakis, New Democracy, since 8 June 2019 (Kyriakos Mitsotakis's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Konstantinos Tasoulas, New Democracy, since 13 March 2025 (Konstantinos Tasoulas's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, picked by parliament, is mostly for show.

How Voting Works

Parties mainly share out seats by their votes, and need at least 3%. The first-placed party can get bonus seats to help it form a government.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 85 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 5.7 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 41% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Greece won independence from the Ottoman Empire in the 1820s. It went through a civil war in the 1940s and a military junta from 1967 to 1974. Voters then abolished the monarchy. A severe debt crisis dominated its politics after 2009.

Who It Stands With

  • NATO: A military alliance. Members promise to defend one another.
  • European Union: European countries that make many of their laws together.
