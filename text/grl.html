How It's Run

Greenland largely runs itself as part of the Kingdom of Denmark. Its own parliament and prime minister handle most things, while Denmark handles defense and foreign policy.

How Voting Works

Parties share out seats in the Inatsisartut by their votes. Greenland also elects two members to Denmark's parliament. Independence is a central question in its politics.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Inuit peoples have lived in Greenland for centuries, and Denmark colonized it from the 1700s. It became part of Denmark in 1953, gained home rule in 1979, and wider self-government in 2009.
