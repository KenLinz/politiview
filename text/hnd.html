Honduras

Central America, about 9,746,117 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Nasry Asfura, National Party of Honduras, since 27 January 2026 (Nasry Asfura's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a president to run the government and a National Congress with one chamber. Most power sits with the national government, not with local areas.

How Voting Works

The president wins with the most votes in one round, with no runoff. Congress seats are shared by party vote share, and voters can pick individual candidates across party lists.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 6.1 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).

How It Got Here

Honduras has been free of Spain since 1821. It had many army governments in the 1900s, and US banana companies had huge influence. Civilian rule came back in 1982. A 2009 coup removed the president, and disputed elections have caused trouble since.
