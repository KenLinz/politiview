World Politics In Plain Words

This browser couldn't start the 3D globe, so you're on the plain version. Everything important is here too.

Politiview makes the world's politics easy to reach. Pick any country to see who's in charge, how its government works, how voting works, and when people next get a say.

We side with no party and we'll never tell you who to vote for. We just want more people to take part. In the US, you can register to vote here. Anywhere else, your national election commission can tell you how.

This is the plain version, made for screen readers, slow connections and anyone who'd rather read. The globe has the same information as a living map.
