Iceland

Northern Europe, about 361,313 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Kristrún Mjöll Frostadóttir, Social Democratic Alliance, since 21 December 2024 (Kristrún Mjöll Frostadóttir's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Halla Tómasdóttir, Independent, since 1 August 2024 (Halla Tómasdóttir's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament called the Althing, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, also elected by voters, is mostly for show.

How Voting Works

Parties share out seats by their votes across several districts. This usually means a coalition.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

Who Takes Part

  • About 81% of registered voters turned out in 2024, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 46% of seats in parliament.

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 7.5 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).

How It Got Here

The Althing, founded in 930, is often called the world's oldest parliament. Iceland was ruled by Norway and then Denmark, and it became a fully independent republic in 1944.

Who It Stands With
