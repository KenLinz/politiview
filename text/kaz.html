Kazakhstan

Central Asia, about 18,513,930 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, Independent, since 20 March 2019 (Kassym-Jomart Tokayev's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Olzhas Bektenov, Independent, since 6 February 2024 (Olzhas Bektenov's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A strong president is in charge, under a system built around Nursultan Nazarbayev, who led from independence until 2019. Presidents now get one seven-year term.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but real opposition parties have struggled to register, and observers say there hasn't been true competition.

Who Takes Part

  • About 54% of registered voters turned out in 2023, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 18% of seats in parliament.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 23 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 6.6 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 72% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Kazakhstan broke from the Soviet Union in 1991. Deadly unrest in January 2022 shook the system and cut Nazarbayev's influence, under the man who followed him, Kassym-Jomart Tokayev.

Who It Stands With

  • Shanghai Cooperation Organisation: A Eurasian political and security group led by China and Russia.
