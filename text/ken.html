Kenya

Eastern Africa, about 52,573,973 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: William Ruto, United Democratic Alliance, since 13 September 2022 (William Ruto's record on Wikidata)

The Next Vote

President, National Assembly and Senate, 10 August 2027.

How It's Run

Voters elect a president, who runs the government, along with a parliament. Since a new constitution in 2010, 47 counties have their own elected governors.

How Voting Works

A president must win over 50% of votes nationally, and at least a quarter of the votes in 24 of the 47 counties. Results are often challenged in court, and the Supreme Court annulled one in 2017.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

  • People here rate their lives 4.7 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 50% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Kenya won independence from Britain in 1963 and had only one party until the early 1990s. Disputed elections in 2007 led to violence. A power-sharing deal and then the 2010 constitution followed.

Who It Stands With
