Kyrgyzstan

Central Asia, about 6,456,900 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Sadyr Japarov, Mekenchil, since 28 January 2021 (Sadyr Japarov's record on Wikidata)

The Next Vote

President, 27 January 2027.

How It's Run

A president holds strong powers, after a 2021 change moved power away from parliament.

How Voting Works

Voters elect 90 lawmakers in three-seat districts, with one vote each. Kyrgyzstan was once the most open country in Central Asia, with real contests. Rights groups say the media and opposition have come under pressure in recent years.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Since independence in 1991, uprisings have brought down its presidents three times, in 2005, 2010 and 2020. That earned it a name for people power.
