South Korea

Eastern Asia, about 51,709,098 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Lee Jae Myung, Democratic Party of Korea, since 4 June 2025 (Lee Jae Myung's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A president, elected by voters, runs the government for one five-year term and can never run again. The National Assembly passes laws and can remove the president.

How Voting Works

The president wins with the most votes, with no runoff. The Assembly uses a mix of local winners and party-share seats. In December 2024, the president declared martial law, and lawmakers voted it down within hours. He was then removed from office, and a new president was elected in 2025.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

After the Korean War, a series of harsh leaders ruled the South, even as its economy boomed. Mass protests in 1987 won the right to vote directly for president. It became one of Asia's strongest democracies.
