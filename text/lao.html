Laos

South-Eastern Asia, about 7,169,455 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Thongloun Sisoulith, Lao People's Revolutionary Party (Thongloun Sisoulith's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Saleumxay Kommasith, Lao People's Revolutionary Party (Saleumxay Kommasith's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

One party, the Lao People's Revolutionary Party, holds all power. No other parties are allowed.

How Voting Works

People vote for the National Assembly, but almost all candidates belong to or are approved by the ruling party.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 5.5 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 76% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Laos was once French land. It became a communist state in 1975, ending its monarchy, after a long civil war linked to the Vietnam War.
