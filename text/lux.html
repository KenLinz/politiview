Luxembourg

Western Europe, about 619,896 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Luc Frieden, Christian Social People's Party, since 17 November 2023 (Luc Frieden's record on Wikidata)
  • Grand Duke: Guillaume V, Grand Duke of Luxembourg, since 3 October 2025 (Guillaume V, Grand Duke of Luxembourg's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister leads the government. A Grand Duke is the ceremonial head of state: Luxembourg is the world's last grand duchy.

How Voting Works

Seats are shared by party vote share, and voters can even spread their votes between candidates of different parties. Voting is compulsory.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • By law, everyone has to vote.

How It Got Here

Luxembourg was once much larger, but bigger powers kept carving it up. It became fully independent in 1867. It helped found what became the European Union.
