How It's Run

Voters elect a president, who runs the government for one six-year term and can never run again. Congress has a Senate and a Chamber of Deputies. Mexico has 31 states plus Mexico City, which has much the same powers.

How Voting Works

The president wins with the most votes, with no runoff. Lawmakers are chosen by a mix: some win local districts, and others get seats based on their party's share of the vote. The single-term rule for presidents is one of the country's firmest political traditions.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 3 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Spain conquered the Aztec Empire in 1521 and ruled Mexico for three centuries. Independence came in 1821. Then came years of chaos, foreign invasions and a long dictatorship. A revolution began in 1910. Its winners formed the party that held the presidency for 71 years. That ended peacefully in 2000, when an opposition candidate won.
