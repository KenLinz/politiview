Marshall Islands

Micronesia, about 58,791 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Hilda Heine, Independent, since 3 January 2024 (Hilda Heine's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, which picks a president. A council of traditional chiefs also advises.

How Voting Works

Voters elect members from local districts, some with one seat and some with several. Citizens living in the US can vote by mail.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

The US tested 67 nuclear weapons here from 1946 to 1958, including at Bikini Atoll, and the effects on people and land continue. It became self-governing in 1986 under a compact with the US.
