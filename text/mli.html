Mali

Western Africa, about 19,658,031 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Assimi Goïta, Independent, since 24 May 2021 (Assimi Goïta's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

The military has ruled since coups in 2020 and 2021, led by General Assimi Goïta. It has ended military ties with France and the UN mission, and works with Russian forces.

How Voting Works

Elections have been delayed again and again. Political parties were shut down in 2025. Goïta was given a five-year term that can be renewed, with no election. The junta says that's needed for security and reform.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • Nobody has to vote.

Who Takes Part

  • About 36% of registered voters turned out in 2020, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 30% of seats in parliament.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • V-Dem: Closed Autocracy (2025)
  • Freedom House: 21 Out Of 100 (2025)
  • Bertelsmann: Hard-Line Autocracy (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 4.6 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 48% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

The ancient Mali Empire was based here. Mali won independence from France in 1960. A 1991 coup led to democracy with many parties. Since 2012, jihadist and Tuareg uprisings have hit the north and center.

Who It Stands With

  • African Union: Nearly every country in Africa, working together on peace and politics.
