Myanmar

South-Eastern Asia, about 54,045,420 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Min Aung Hlaing, Union Solidarity and Development Party, since 1 February 2021 (Min Aung Hlaing's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

The military seized power in a February 2021 coup, jailing elected leader Aung San Suu Kyi. Fighting against armed resistance groups continues across much of the country.

How Voting Works

The junta held a phased election from December 2025 into 2026, but major parties were dissolved and many areas couldn't vote. Many governments and observers rejected it as a sham.

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 4 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 4.3 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 92% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Independent from Britain in 1948, Burma fell to army rule from 1962. A partial opening after 2011 let Suu Kyi's party win a landslide in 2015. The 2017 military campaign against Rohingya Muslims drove hundreds of thousands out of the country.
