Mauritania

Western Africa, about 4,525,696 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Mohamed Ould Ghazouani, Equity Party, since 1 August 2019 (Mohamed Ould Ghazouani's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Moctar Ould Djay, Equity Party, since 2 August 2024 (Moctar Ould Djay's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A president, elected by voters, runs the government along with a parliament. Army leaders have played a big part in politics since independence.

How Voting Works

A president needs a majority, or there's a runoff. The 2019 vote was the first handover between two elected presidents, though both were former army generals. The president was re-elected in 2024.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

  • About 55% of registered voters turned out in 2024, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 23% of seats in parliament.

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 38 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 4.5 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).

How It Got Here

Independent from France in 1960, it has seen several coups. It formally abolished slavery in 1981 and made it a crime in 2007, but rights groups say it persists, and it is often described as the last country to abolish it.

Who It Stands With

  • African Union: Nearly every country in Africa, working together on peace and politics.
  • Arab League: Arabic-speaking countries that coordinate on politics.
