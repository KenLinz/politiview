Malaysia

South-Eastern Asia, about 31,949,777 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Anwar Ibrahim, People's Justice Party, since 24 November 2022 (Anwar Ibrahim's record on Wikidata)
  • King: Ibrahim Iskandar of Johor, since 31 January 2024 (Ibrahim Iskandar of Johor's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. It has an unusual monarchy: nine state sultans take turns as king, for five years each. It's also a federal country.

How Voting Works

Whoever gets the most votes in each area wins. Rural seats tend to have fewer voters than urban ones. Politics is shaped strongly by race and religion.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 53 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 6.0 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 58% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Independent from Britain in 1957, Malaysia was governed by one coalition for 61 years, until voters ended that run in 2018. Governments have shifted several times since.
