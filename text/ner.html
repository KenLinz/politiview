Niger

Western Africa, about 23,310,715 people

Who's In Charge

  • Transitional President: Abdourahamane Tiani, since 28 July 2023 (Abdourahamane Tiani's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Ali Lamine Zeine, National Movement for the Development of Society, since 8 August 2023 (Ali Lamine Zeine's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

The military took power in a July 2023 coup, removing elected President Mohamed Bazoum, who has been detained. The junta has since broken with France and moved closer to Russia.

How Voting Works

No elections are planned for now. The junta announced a five-year transition in 2025.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • Nobody has to vote.

Who Takes Part

  • About 63% of registered voters turned out in 2021, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 20% of seats in parliament.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • V-Dem: Closed Autocracy (2025)
  • Freedom House: 27 Out Of 100 (2025)
  • Bertelsmann: Hard-Line Autocracy (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 4.9 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).

How It Got Here

Independent from France in 1960, Niger has had several coups. Its 2021 vote was the first peaceful handover of power between elected presidents.

Who It Stands With

  • African Union: Nearly every country in Africa, working together on peace and politics.
