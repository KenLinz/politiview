Nicaragua

Central America, about 6,545,502 people

Who's In Charge

  • Co-Presidents: Daniel Ortega, Sandinista National Liberation Front, since 10 January 2007 (Daniel Ortega's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

On paper, voters elect a president and a National Assembly. In real life, President Daniel Ortega and his wife, Rosario Murillo, hold almost all the power. Changes to the constitution now make them co-presidents.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but top opposition candidates were jailed before the 2021 vote. Many observers from other countries rejected the results. Thousands of critics have been forced to leave or have lost their citizenship.

How It Got Here

The Somoza family ruled as dictators for over 40 years, until the left-wing Sandinista revolution of 1979. A US-backed rebel force, the Contras, then fought the Sandinistas through the 1980s. Voters removed Ortega in 1990. He returned to power in 2007 and has since steadily dismantled limits on it.
