Norway

Northern Europe, about 5,347,896 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Jonas Gahr Støre, Norwegian Labour Party, since 14 October 2021 (Jonas Gahr Støre's record on Wikidata)
  • King: Haakon VIII of Norway, since 28 August 2026 (Haakon VIII of Norway's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament called the Storting, and the prime minister leads the government. The king's role is ceremonial.

How Voting Works

Seats are shared by party vote share, with extra seats to even things out across the country. Parliament can't be dissolved early, so elections fall every four years.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

  • People here rate their lives 7.2 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 94% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Norway wrote its constitution in 1814, one of the oldest still in force, and was then joined with Sweden under a shared king. It became fully independent in 1905.
