Nepal

Southern Asia, about 28,608,710 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Balen Shah, Rastriya Swatantra Party (Balen Shah's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Ram Chandra Poudel, Nepali Congress, since 1 January 2023 (Ram Chandra Poudel's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and a prime minister runs the government. A president is ceremonial. Nepal was a Hindu kingdom until 2008, and it's now a federal republic.

How Voting Works

A mixed system: 165 members win local areas, and 110 get seats by party vote share. Youth-led protests in September 2025 toppled the government, and a new election in March 2026 was won by the Rastriya Swatantra Party.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

Who Takes Part

  • About 61% of registered voters turned out in 2022, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 35% of seats in parliament.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 5.2 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 50% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

A Maoist rebellion from 1996 to 2006 ended with a peace deal. That led to the end of the 240-year-old monarchy in 2008, and a new constitution in 2015. Governments have rarely lasted long since.
