Oman

Western Asia, about 4,974,986 people

Who's In Charge

  • Sultan: Haitham bin Tarik Al Said, since 11 January 2020 (Haitham bin Tarik Al Said's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

The sultan holds all final power, serving as head of state, prime minister and commander of the armed forces. An elected council only advises.

How Voting Works

Citizens elect one part of a council that gives advice. It can't overrule the sultan, and parties aren't allowed.

  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Oman was once a trading empire that reached East Africa. Sultan Qaboos, who ruled from 1970 to 2020, opened it up and modernized it. It's known for quietly helping rivals in the region talk.
