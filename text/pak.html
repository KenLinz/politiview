Pakistan

Southern Asia, about 216,565,318 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Shehbaz Sharif, Pakistan Muslim League (N), since 11 April 2022 (Shehbaz Sharif's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Asif Ali Zardari, Pakistan Peoples Party, since 1 January 2024 (Asif Ali Zardari's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a National Assembly, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, picked by lawmakers, is mostly for show. The army has ruled directly several times, and many observers say it stays very powerful behind the scenes.

How Voting Works

Most seats go to whoever gets the most votes in each area. Others are saved for women and religious minorities and shared among the parties. Former prime minister Imran Khan was jailed before the 2024 election, and his party lost its symbol, so its candidates ran as independents. His party and many observers disputed the result, and officials defended it.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Pakistan was created in 1947 as a homeland for South Asia's Muslims, when British India was partitioned. East Pakistan broke away as Bangladesh in 1971. Generals have seized power several times, and no prime minister has ever completed a full term.
