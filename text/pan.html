Panama

Central America, about 4,246,439 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: José Raúl Mulino, Realizing Goals, since 1 July 2024 (José Raúl Mulino's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a president for one five-year term, and a National Assembly with one chamber. A president can't run again right away.

How Voting Works

The president wins with the most votes in one round, with no runoff. The 71 Assembly members are chosen in local districts, some electing one member and some several.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.

How It Got Here

Panama separated from Colombia in 1903 with US backing, and the US then built the canal and controlled the land around it. Military ruler Manuel Noriega was removed by a US invasion in 1989. Panama took over full control of the canal at the end of 1999.
