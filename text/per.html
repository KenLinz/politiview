Peru

South America, about 32,510,453 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Keiko Fujimori, Popular Force, since 28 July 2026 (Keiko Fujimori's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a president and a Congress with two chambers. Congress can remove a president for 'moral incapacity'. It has done so again and again, which has led to many presidents in just a few years.

How Voting Works

A president needs a majority, or the top two go to a runoff. Congress seats are shared by party vote share. Voting is compulsory.

How It Got Here

Peru was the heart of the Inca Empire before the Spanish conquest. It saw long military rule, and a brutal war with Shining Path rebels in the 1980s and 90s. President Alberto Fujimori was later jailed, and the country has lurched between short-lived governments since.
