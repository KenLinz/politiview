Philippines

South-Eastern Asia, about 108,116,615 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Bongbong Marcos, Partido Federal ng Pilipinas, since 30 June 2022 (Bongbong Marcos's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a president, who runs the government for one six-year term. Congress has a Senate, elected nationwide, and a House of Representatives. Observers say powerful political families control many areas.

How Voting Works

The president wins with the most votes, with no runoff, and the vice president is elected separately, so they can be from rival camps. Most House members win local areas, and others come from party lists.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 3 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 58 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 6.2 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 83% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Spain ruled for over 300 years, followed by the US until independence in 1946. Ferdinand Marcos ruled as a dictator from 1965 until the peaceful People Power uprising of 1986. His son won the presidency in 2022.
