Palau

Micronesia, about 18,008 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Surangel Whipps Jr., Independent, since 21 January 2021 (Surangel Whipps Jr.'s record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a president, who runs the government, along with a Congress with two chambers. Traditional chiefs also give respected advice. There are no political parties.

How Voting Works

A primary narrows the field for president, and everyone votes for people rather than parties.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Japan and then the US once ran Palau. It became independent in 1994 under a deal with the US.
