Papua New Guinea

Melanesia, about 8,776,109 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: James Marape, Pangu Party, since 30 May 2019 (James Marape's record on Wikidata)
  • King: Charles III, since 8 September 2022 (Charles III's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. King Charles is formally head of state, with a governor-general acting for him. Its people speak over 800 languages, more than any other country.

How Voting Works

Voters rank up to three candidates, a method called limited preferential voting. Most candidates are independents or belong to small parties. So coalitions keep changing, and votes to remove governments are common.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Germany and Britain split up this land, and Australia later ran it until independence in 1975. Bougainville fought a long war to break away. In a 2019 vote nearly all its voters chose independence. The result isn't binding, and the national parliament has the final say.
