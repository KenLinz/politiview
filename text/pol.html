Poland

Eastern Europe, about 37,970,874 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Donald Tusk, Civic Coalition, since 13 December 2023 (Donald Tusk's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Karol Nawrocki, Independent, since 6 August 2025 (Karol Nawrocki's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect the Sejm, and the prime minister runs the government. A directly elected president can veto laws, which leads to real conflict when the two come from opposing camps.

How Voting Works

Sejm seats are shared by party vote share within districts, with a 5% minimum for parties. The Senate is elected by first-past-the-post. The president is elected in two rounds, if needed.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Poland's neighbors divided it up in the late 1700s, and it vanished from the map for over a century. It came back in 1918, was invaded in 1939, and fell under Soviet-backed communism after the war. The Solidarity trade union helped force partly free elections in 1989. That set off the fall of communism across Eastern Europe.
