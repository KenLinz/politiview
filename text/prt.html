Portugal

Southern Europe, about 10,269,417 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Luís Montenegro, Social Democratic Party, since 2 April 2024 (Luís Montenegro's record on Wikidata)
  • President: António José Seguro, Socialist Party, since 9 March 2026 (António José Seguro's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister needs its support to run the government. A president, also elected by voters, has real but limited powers, like blocking laws and shutting down parliament.

How Voting Works

Parliament's seats are shared by party vote share. The president is elected in two rounds, if needed.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

  • About 48% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Portugal was a kingdom for centuries with a huge global empire. It became a republic in 1910 and fell under a dictatorship from 1926. An almost bloodless army uprising, the Carnation Revolution, ended that in 1974. Its colonies won independence soon after.
