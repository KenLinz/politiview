Qatar

Western Asia, about 2,832,067 people

Who's In Charge

  • Emir: Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, since 25 June 2013 (Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani, since 1 January 2023 (Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

The emir, from the Al Thani family, holds all final power. An advisory council assists him. Political parties aren't allowed.

How Voting Works

A first council election took place in 2021. But in a 2024 vote, citizens agreed to end those elections and go back to an appointed council. Most people living here are foreign workers with no vote.

  • Nobody has to vote.

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 6.4 out of 10 (2018, World Happiness Report).

How It Got Here

Independent from Britain in 1971, Qatar became one of the world's richest countries per person from natural gas. It hosted the 2022 World Cup.
