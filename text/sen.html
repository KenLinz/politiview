Senegal

Western Africa, about 16,296,364 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Bassirou Diomaye Faye, Independent, since 2 April 2024 (Bassirou Diomaye Faye's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Ahmadou Al Aminou Lo, Independent (Ahmadou Al Aminou Lo's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A president, elected by voters, has strong powers, and a prime minister helps lead the government. Senegal has never had a successful military coup, which is rare in the region.

How Voting Works

A president needs a majority, or there's a runoff. In 2024, courts and protests blocked an attempt to delay the vote. Opposition candidate Bassirou Diomaye Faye won in the first round.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

Who Takes Part

  • About 61% of registered voters turned out in 2024, as officially reported.
  • Women hold about 41% of seats in parliament.

What Researchers Say

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • Freedom House: 70 Out Of 100 (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 4.8 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).
  • About 57% said they trust their national government (2020, Wellcome Global Monitor).

How It Got Here

Independent from France in 1960, Senegal was led by the poet Léopold Senghor. It's seen peaceful handovers of power in 2000, 2012 and 2024.

Who It Stands With

  • African Union: Nearly every country in Africa, working together on peace and politics.
