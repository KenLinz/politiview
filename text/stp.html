São Tomé and Príncipe

Middle Africa, about 215,056 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Américo Ramos, Independent, since 12 January 2025 (Américo Ramos's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Carlos Vila Nova, Independent, since 2 October 2021 (Carlos Vila Nova's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

A president, elected by voters, and a prime minister share power. The country has held peaceful, competitive elections.

How Voting Works

A president needs a majority, or there's a runoff. The 55-seat National Assembly is elected for four years, with seats shared by party vote share.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

These islands were a Portuguese colony of big farms, and became independent in 1975. Elections with many parties began in 1991.
