Syria

Western Asia, about 17,070,135 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Ahmed al-Sharaa, Independent, since 29 January 2025 (Ahmed al-Sharaa's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Syria is between governments. Rebels overthrew Bashar al-Assad in December 2024, and their leader, Ahmed al-Sharaa, became temporary president. A temporary constitution gives him wide powers for five years.

How Voting Works

Critics say elections under Assad were staged. A new assembly was chosen in 2025. Local groups set up by a government committee filled 140 seats, with no public vote, and the president appointed 70. Full national elections have been promised for later.

  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Independent from France in 1946, Syria was ruled by the Assad family from 1970. Peaceful protests in 2011 were met with force, and a civil war followed that displaced about half the population.
