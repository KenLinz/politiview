Chad

Middle Africa, about 15,946,876 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Mahamat Déby, Patriotic Salvation Movement (Mahamat Déby's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Allamaye Halina, Independent (Allamaye Halina's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Mahamat Déby took over from his father, who was killed fighting rebels in 2021. He first led through a military council, and was declared the winner of a disputed vote for president in 2024.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but opposition figures say the 2024 vote was unfair. Official results gave Déby 61 percent. A key rival, Yaya Dillo, was killed before it. Officials called that a gun battle, and critics called it an assassination.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Independent from France in 1960, Chad has seen long civil wars and frequent rebellions. Idriss Déby ruled for 30 years, from 1990 to 2021.
