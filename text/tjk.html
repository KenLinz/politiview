Tajikistan

Central Asia, about 9,321,018 people

Who's In Charge

  • President: Emomali Rahmon, People's Democratic Party of Tajikistan (Emomali Rahmon's record on Wikidata)
  • Prime Minister: Kokhir Rasulzoda, People's Democratic Party of Tajikistan, since 23 November 2013 (Kokhir Rasulzoda's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Emomali Rahmon has ruled since 1992, and he's been given the title 'Leader of the Nation', with no limit on his terms. His son leads the upper house and is widely seen as a successor.

How Voting Works

Elections are held, but observers say they aren't real contests. The main Islamic opposition party was banned in 2015.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

Different experts judge countries differently, so here are three.

  • V-Dem: Electoral Autocracy (2025)
  • Freedom House: 5 Out Of 100 (2025)
  • Bertelsmann: Hard-Line Autocracy (2025)

What Surveys Say

  • People here rate their lives 5.6 out of 10 (2025, World Happiness Report).

How It Got Here

After independence from the Soviets in 1991, Tajikistan fell into a civil war from 1992 to 1997, which ended with a peace deal. Rahmon has since built a tightly controlled state, according to critics.

Who It Stands With

  • Shanghai Cooperation Organisation: A Eurasian political and security group led by China and Russia.
