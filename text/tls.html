East Timor

South-Eastern Asia, about 1,293,119 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Xanana Gusmão, National Congress for Timorese Reconstruction, since 1 January 2023 (Xanana Gusmão's record on Wikidata)
  • President: José Ramos-Horta, National Congress for Timorese Reconstruction, since 20 May 2022 (José Ramos-Horta's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, also elected by voters, helps keep the balance.

How Voting Works

Seats are shared by party vote share across the whole country, and the president is elected in two rounds, if needed.

  • Voting age: 17.
  • People vote about every 5 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Timor-Leste was a Portuguese colony. Indonesia invaded in 1975 and held it for 24 years. In a vote run by the UN in 1999, people chose independence, which began in 2002.
