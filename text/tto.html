Trinidad and Tobago

Caribbean, about 1,394,973 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Kamla Persad-Bissessar, United National Congress, since 1 May 2025 (Kamla Persad-Bissessar's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Christine Kangaloo, People's National Movement, since 20 March 2023 (Christine Kangaloo's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, picked by lawmakers, is head of state, but only for show.

How Voting Works

Local areas elect members by first-past-the-post. Politics has often split between people of African and Indian descent.

How It Got Here

Spain and then Britain ruled these two islands. Workers were brought from India after slavery ended, shaping today's mixed society. Independence came in 1962, and a republic in 1976.
