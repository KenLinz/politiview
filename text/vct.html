Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

Caribbean, about 110,589 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Godwin Friday, New Democratic Party (Godwin Friday's record on Wikidata)
  • King: Charles III, since 8 September 2022 (Charles III's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister leads the government. King Charles is the ceremonial head of state. Voters rejected becoming a republic in 2009.

How Voting Works

Local areas elect members by first-past-the-post, and some seats are appointed.

How It Got Here

The Garifuna people fought off colonizers for a long time. Britain then took control and sent many of them away in 1797. Independence came in 1979.
