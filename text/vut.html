Vanuatu

Melanesia, about 299,882 people

Who's In Charge

  • Prime Minister: Jotham Napat, Leaders Party of Vanuatu (Jotham Napat's record on Wikidata)
  • President: Nikenike Vurobaravu, Vanua'aku Pati, since 23 July 2022 (Nikenike Vurobaravu's record on Wikidata)

How It's Run

Voters elect a parliament, and the prime minister runs the government. A president, picked by a special group, is mostly for show. Votes to remove governments have often brought them down.

How Voting Works

Most seats are filled by voters in multi-member areas, where each voter gets one vote, and the rest in single-seat areas. Many small parties compete.

  • Voting age: 18.
  • People vote about every 4 years.
  • Nobody has to vote.

How It Got Here

Britain and France ran these islands jointly from 1906, with two sets of laws and police. Vanuatu became independent in 1980.

Who It Stands With

  • The Commonwealth: Mostly former parts of the British Empire, tied by history and language.
